# CHI-SQUARE RULES

Goodness of fit:
H0 specifies category probabilities p_k0.
Expected count E_k=n p_k0.
Statistic=sum (O-E)^2/E.
df=K-1 when probabilities are fully specified.
Right-tail test.

Independence:
Two categorical variables.
H0: variables independent.
H1: associated / not independent.
Expected count=(row total * column total)/grand total.
df=(r-1)(c-1).
Right-tail test.

Do not confuse GOF with independence.
